How We Work

A rigorous process. A personal approach.

We work on a retained, relationship basis. Every mandate gets our full attention — not a database search and a shortlist.

01

Define the Brief

We spend time with your leadership team to understand not just the role requirements, but the culture, the challenges, and what makes this opportunity distinctive for a high-calibre candidate.

02

Source & Map

Active sourcing through our networks — not passive database matching. We approach candidates who aren't necessarily looking, because the best leaders rarely are.

03

Vet & Assess

Rigorous interviews, reference checks, and assessment against the role's success criteria. We present a small, high-quality shortlist — not a volume of profiles to wade through.

04

Place & Partner

We support the offer, negotiation, and onboarding — and stay in contact with both client and placed leader to ensure a successful transition and long-term fit.
