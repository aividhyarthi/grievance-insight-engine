He also found out, within a few hours, that a man managing his own midlife symptoms needs to think about a fast very differently than he expected.

## Why are husbands fasting on Karwa Chauth now?

**Karwa Chauth has traditionally been observed by wives for their husbands' long life and wellbeing, but a visible shift, especially in urban, dual-income households, has more husbands choosing to fast too, as a gesture of reciprocity and shared hardship rather than a one-sided ritual.** This isn't a replacement for the tradition's meaning, most couples who do this say it hasn't changed the ritual's significance for the wife, it's added a second layer: a husband showing he understands what a full day without food or water actually feels like, rather than simply buying a gift and watching from the sidelines.

**For many midlife couples, this shift coincides with a broader renegotiation of who carries what in a marriage, and it often lands at exactly the same years a man is quietly dealing with his own andropause symptoms.** A husband fasting isn't just a nice gesture in the abstract, it's frequently one data point in a marriage where both partners are learning to be more visible to each other about what their bodies are going through in midlife.

## Is it safe for a man with andropause symptoms to fast for a full day?

For most healthy men, yes, a single dawn-to-moonrise fast without food or water is manageable, but midlife changes make a few things worth planning for.

**Falling testosterone is linked to fatigue, irritability, and in some men, blood sugar swings, and all three can be temporarily sharper on a day with no food or water, particularly by late afternoon.** This doesn't make fasting unsafe, it makes the day more noticeable in a way a man in his 20s might not have experienced the same fast. Knowing this in advance, rather than being surprised by an irritable mood or a pounding headache at 4pm, makes the day considerably easier to manage for both partners.

### What actually helps on the day

**Eating a protein and fat-heavy meal before sunrise, rather than a carbohydrate-heavy one, keeps blood sugar steadier through the day and reduces the afternoon energy crash that many men notice more sharply in midlife than they did a decade earlier.** Good pre-dawn choices include paneer, eggs, nuts, and full-fat dairy rather than only rice or sweets, which spike and then drop blood sugar quickly. Staying out of direct heat, avoiding strenuous exercise on the fast day itself, and resting when possible all reduce strain considerably.

## Does fasting together change anything about managing symptoms as a couple?

Often, yes, in a way that goes beyond the single day itself.

**A husband who fasts alongside his wife, even once, frequently reports a shift in how comfortable he feels telling her about his own fatigue, mood changes, or low energy the rest of the year, because the day creates a shared reference point for physical discomfort that didn't exist before.** It's a small, concrete experience that can open a conversation neither partner quite knew how to start otherwise, particularly in households where a man's andropause symptoms have gone unspoken out of embarrassment or simply not having the right moment to bring it up.

**For couples where the wife is also navigating her own menopause transition, a reciprocal fast can become a useful, low-pressure way to acknowledge that both of you are dealing with midlife hormonal change, without turning the conversation into a competition over who has it harder.** The goal isn't symmetry, it's visibility: each partner seeing a little more of what the other is carrying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it safe for a man with low testosterone to fast on Karwa Chauth?**
For most men without diabetes, kidney disease, or heart conditions, a single day's fast is generally safe, though fatigue and irritability linked to andropause may feel more pronounced. Men on regular medication, especially for blood pressure or diabetes, should confirm timing with their doctor beforehand.

**Does fasting affect testosterone levels directly?**
A single day's fast is unlikely to meaningfully change testosterone levels on its own. Prolonged or repeated fasting without adequate nutrition can affect hormonal health over time, which is a separate consideration from a once-a-year observance.

**What should I eat before sunrise if I'm planning to fast?**
Favour protein and healthy fats, such as paneer, eggs, nuts, or dairy, over carbohydrate-heavy or sugary foods, since they keep blood sugar steadier through the day and reduce the energy crash some men notice by late afternoon.

**Is reciprocal fasting actually common now, or still rare?**
It remains a minority practice overall, but it has visibly grown among younger and midlife urban couples over the past decade, often framed less as changing the tradition and more as adding a gesture of partnership alongside it.
