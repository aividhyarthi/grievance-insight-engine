Communications

Sustained, strategic communications that shape how your market perceives your brand and your leadership. We place your story in the publications your customers, investors, and peers trust most.

Start the Conversation

Earned media that builds credibility you cannot buy

A press release is not a PR strategy. Genuine brand authority is built through consistent, intelligent storytelling sustained over time, relationships with editors and journalists who trust your voice, and narratives that hold up under scrutiny.

We work with ambitious brands and leadership teams to craft the narratives that define their market position, secure Tier-1 placements in print, digital, and broadcast, and manage communications through both growth and complexity. Our team includes former journalists and senior communications professionals with deep relationships across India's leading media landscape.

  • Strategic narrative development and message architecture
  • Tier-1 media outreach, pitching, and placement
  • Executive thought leadership and spokesperson positioning
  • Press release strategy, creation, and wire distribution
  • Crisis communications and reputation issues management
  • Investor and board stakeholder communications
  • Awards and industry recognition programme management
  • Broadcast and podcast media opportunity development
01

Narrative & Message Architecture

We develop your brand story from first principles, core messages, spokesperson talking points, and a narrative framework that every communications interaction builds from. This is the foundation. Without it, even great media relationships produce inconsistent coverage.

02

Media Relations & Placement

Active outreach and relationship management with journalists, editors, and broadcast producers across India's top business, trade, and consumer media. We pitch, we follow up, and we close, securing coverage that reflects your brand's real stature.

03

Measurement & Business Impact

Monthly reporting on coverage quality, share of voice, sentiment trends, and audience reach, always linked to the business outcomes that matter: investor sentiment, talent attraction, pipeline velocity, and brand perception.

How We Build Executive Visibility

A 12-month thought leadership programme, not a one-off campaign

01

Positioning Audit

We assess your executive's current media presence, identify the whitespace in your market's conversation, and define the three to five topics your leadership team should own. Most executives are underexposed relative to their actual expertise.

02

Editorial Calendar Development

A rolling 12-month calendar of op-eds, comment opportunities, podcast pitches, and speaking nominations, timed to news cycles, industry events, and the business milestones that make your story timely and relevant.

03

Content Creation & Media Outreach

We write, refine, and pitch. Every op-ed, comment, and executive interview is prepared to your voice and approved before submission. We manage all media relationships and handle follow-up so your time cost is minimal.

04

Amplification & Measurement

Each placement is amplified across your LinkedIn, company channels, and email communications for maximum audience reach. Monthly reports track volume, quality, sentiment, and share of voice against your peer set.
