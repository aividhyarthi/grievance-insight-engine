Off-the-shelf AI tools solve generic problems. The organisations gaining a real edge are those with AI agents built specifically for their data, their processes, and their people. We build those agents.

Our practice brings together product thinking, engineering rigour, and deep understanding of enterprise workflows. We work closely with founders, CTOs, COOs, and operations leaders to identify the highest-impact automation opportunities and build agents that deliver measurable results from day one.

01

Discovery & Architecture

We map your workflows, audit your existing systems, and identify the highest-value automation opportunities. Then we design an agent architecture that integrates cleanly with your technical environment and scales with your growth.

02

Build, Test & Iterate

Development happens in close collaboration with your team. We build iteratively, test against real-world scenarios, and document everything to ensure your people can operate and extend the agent independently.

We manage deployment, run team training sessions, and monitor agent performance post-launch. As your business evolves, we iterate. Most engagements move into a retained optimisation programme after initial delivery.

What We Build

AI agents built for real enterprise problems

Every agent we build is scoped, designed, and tested against the specific constraints of your business. Here are the categories we work in most.

Sales & Revenue

Lead Intelligence Agent

Monitors inbound signals, scores and qualifies leads against your ICP, and routes them to the right sales rep with full context, before a human ever touches the record.

Customer Experience

Support & Resolution Agent

Handles Tier-1 support queries, resolves issues against your knowledge base, escalates complex cases intelligently, and logs everything back into your CRM.

Operations

Workflow Orchestration Agent

Manages multi-step internal approvals, procurement processes, and cross-team handoffs. Replaces manual coordination with an agent that tracks, nudges, and escalates automatically.

Knowledge Management

Internal Knowledge Agent

Gives your team instant access to policies, SOPs, product documentation, and institutional knowledge through a conversational interface connected to your existing documents and systems.

Finance & Compliance

Document Processing Agent

Extracts, classifies, and summarises data from contracts, invoices, and compliance documents. Reduces manual review time by 70% or more for document-heavy teams.

Marketing & Content

Content Intelligence Agent

Monitors market signals, competitor activity, and search trends. Surfaces insights and draft briefs for your content team. Keeps your editorial pipeline fed with the right intelligence.
